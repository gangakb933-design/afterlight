# Project Afterlight

**Team Echonova** · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · "Abandoned but Not Forgotten"

Live site: https://gangakb933-design.github.io/afterlight/

*Abandoned, but not forgotten.* A free, mobile-friendly, cinematic educational website about the machines humanity left on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space, and the stories they still tell.

## What is inside
- Nine missions across three destinations (Moon, Mars, Deep space), each told in five chapters: Arrival, The Mission, The Evidence, The Last Signal, What Remains.
- Mission status labels: Silent, Parked, Grounded, Still in use, Still talking.
- Interactive timeline, location map, searchable glossary, mission quizzes, Explorer Passport with stamps, certificate, image lightbox, story mode with Listen, share.
- Reconnect the Signal: a fictional tuning game. The decoded message is fiction; the fact shown with it is NASA-sourced.
- Guardian Run: a fictional space-shooter game. Drag to fly, protect an old machine from drifting debris, beat one boss in each world (Moon, Mars, Deep space) and unlock a real mission fact. It has sound, pause, three difficulty levels and combo scoring, and earns a Guardian badge on the Passport page. The enemies, bosses and messages are fiction; the facts are NASA-sourced.
- Image archive with all cinematic pictures.
- Sound toggle (ambient hum, off by default). Reduced-motion supported.

## Facts, images and credits
- Facts are based on NASA pages; every mission lists its sources. The site is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with NASA.
- Real NASA photographs appear on mission pages with credits.
- The cinematic pictures (hero, destination tiles, card and banner images, chapter strips, image archive) are AI-generated illustrations made by the team with ChatGPT. They are not NASA photographs and do not show the exact spacecraft. The Lunar Roving Vehicle, Viking 1 and Ingenuity mission pictures are illustrations drawn for this project.

## AI tools
Website and game code written with help from Claude (Anthropic). Cinematic images generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI). The video voiceover is AI-generated. The team chose the idea, the story and the features, and checks the facts.

## Files
`index.html` holds the whole site, with the AI illustrations (hero and Moon, Mars and Deep space pictures) embedded inside it. The NASA photos are separate files: `apollo-11.jpg`, `surveyor-3.jpg`, `opportunity.jpg`, `spirit.jpg`, `sojourner.jpg`, `voyager-1.jpg`.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish the repository with GitHub Pages (branch main, root).
